📖 InstaShop
InstaShop is a platform that enables individuals to create their own independent online stores, making it easy for sellers to showcase their products to a wide audience. With intuitive setup and customizable options, InstaShop helps businesses of all sizes launch their e-commerce presence quickly and efficiently.

🚀 Features
Easy Store Setup: Create your store in minutes with a simple, user-friendly interface.
Customizable Storefront: Personalize your store's appearance to match your brand.
Product Management: Add, edit, and organize products effortlessly.
Secure Checkout: Offer a seamless and secure shopping experience for customers.
Analytics Dashboard: Track sales, customer activity, and more with detailed insights.
Responsive Design: Stores look great on any device, from desktops to smartphones.
🛠️ Technologies Used
React, Next.js, Tailwind CSS

Deployment: Vercel
